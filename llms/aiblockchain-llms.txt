# Ai-Blockchain

> Ai-Blockchain (AI BlockChain, AIBC) is a Hoboken, New Jersey enterprise blockchain and artificial-intelligence
> software company founded in 2014 by Drew Hingorani and Stephen Reed. It builds a patented private distributed
> ledger (US Patent 10,579,974) that it describes as gas-free, energy efficient and immutable, with AI agents
> managing the chain. It sells enterprise engagements and consulting, not a self-serve developer product.
